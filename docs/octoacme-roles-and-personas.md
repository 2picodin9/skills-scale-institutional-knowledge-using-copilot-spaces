# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Business or Product Owner

### Role Summary
The Business or Product Owner translates business priorities into clear outcomes, scope, and acceptance criteria. This role keeps the team aligned to customer value and the operating goals of the organization.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives, feature intent, and success measures
- Prioritize work based on value, urgency, and dependencies
- Validate solutions with stakeholders and end users
- Accept or reject delivered outcomes based on agreed criteria
- Clarify trade-offs between scope, timeline, and quality

### How this role interacts with existing personas
- Works closely with the Project Manager to sequence priorities and communicate business decisions
- Aligns with the Product Manager on roadmap direction and outcome measures
- Partner with Developers and Technical Leads to confirm feasibility and expected business impact
- Coordinates with stakeholders to ensure the delivered solution meets operational and customer needs

### Goals
- Keep delivery focused on value and business outcomes
- Reduce ambiguity about priority and expected outcomes
- Improve stakeholder confidence in decisions and trade-offs

---

## Delivery or Program Manager

### Role Summary
The Delivery or Program Manager coordinates work across teams, milestones, and dependencies to help large or multi-stream initiatives stay on track.

### Responsibilities
- Manage delivery plans across related workstreams or projects
- Identify and track dependencies, resource constraints, and cross-team risks
- Coordinate milestone readiness, escalations, and recovery plans
- Support governance and regular progress reporting to leadership
- Help teams balance delivery speed with operational stability

### How this role interacts with existing personas
- Works with Project Managers to align schedules, resource plans, and risk responses
- Supports Product Owners and stakeholders by translating strategic priorities into execution plans
- Collaborates with Technical Leads and Developers to identify delivery bottlenecks and dependency risks
- Escalates issues that require decisions or sponsorship beyond a single team

### Goals
- Improve cross-team coordination and predictability
- Reduce delivery friction across related initiatives
- Ensure risks and dependencies are visible before they become blockers

---

## Technical Lead or Solution Architect

### Role Summary
The Technical Lead or Solution Architect provides technical direction, makes architecture decisions, and ensures that the solution remains feasible, secure, and sustainable.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical approach, design constraints, and integration patterns
- Review implementation choices with Developers and stakeholders
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and architectural trade-offs
- Ensure the solution aligns with standards, maintainability, and operational needs
- Guide technical problem solving during execution

### How this role interacts with existing personas
- Partners with Developers to translate requirements into implementation decisions
- Works with Project Managers and Delivery Managers to assess schedule, dependency, and staffing impacts
- Provides risk and feasibility input to Product Owners and stakeholders
- Coordinates with Security and Operations representatives to ensure compliance and readiness

### Goals
- Maintain technical quality, scalability, and maintainability
- Align engineering work to business objectives and delivery constraints
- Reduce technical uncertainty before and during implementation

---

## Security, Privacy, or Compliance Representative

### Role Summary
This persona ensures that projects meet required controls, security expectations, and regulatory obligations throughout the delivery lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Review requirements for security, privacy, and compliance implications
- Validate controls, evidence, and risk mitigation activities
- Promote secure-by-default practices in design and delivery
- Support review of issues, incident impact, and remediation ownership
- Help define approval gates for release or deployment readiness

### How this role interacts with existing personas
- Works with Technical Leads and Developers to review design, implementation, and controls
- Coordinates with Project Managers and Delivery Managers on risk, milestone timing, and remediation plans
- Supports Product Owners and stakeholders on governance and accountability expectations
- Partners with Operations and Release roles to confirm readiness before production changes

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk in delivery
- Ensure policy requirements are considered early and continuously
- Build trust through clear controls and transparent risk handling

---

## Operations or Platform Representative

### Role Summary
The Operations or Platform Representative ensures that a solution can be supported, monitored, deployed, and maintained after release.

### Responsibilities
- Define deployment, support, and monitoring expectations
- Validate operational readiness, observability, and rollback plans
- Support service ownership, capacity, and performance considerations
- Coordinate handoff from delivery to ongoing support and operations
- Identify operational risks and maintenance requirements early

### How this role interacts with existing personas
- Works with Technical Leads and Developers to confirm deployment and supportability requirements
- Coordinates with Project Managers to sequence operational readiness activities
- Supports Release and Quality roles in validating readiness prior to production deployment
- Helps Product Owners understand long-term operational impact and support costs

### Goals
- Ensure products are deployable, observable, and supportable
- Reduce post-release disruption and operational surprises
- Improve the sustainability of delivered solutions

---

## Quality or Test Lead

### Role Summary
The Quality or Test Lead defines how quality is measured, validated, and communicated across delivery milestones.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality standards, test strategy, and acceptance criteria
- Coordinate test planning, release validation, and defect triage
- Identify quality risks and support corrective action
- Track defect severity, resolution status, and acceptance evidence
- Provide clear quality signals to project leadership and stakeholders

### How this role interacts with existing personas
- Works with Developers to ensure build quality and test coverage are maintained
- Supports Product Owners and Project Managers with risk-informed readiness decisions
- Partners with Technical Leads and Operations to validate production-readiness and customer impact
- Helps Release roles confirm that quality gates are satisfied before deployment

### Goals
- Improve confidence in delivered outcomes
- Reduce escaped defects and rework
- Make quality status visible and actionable for decision makers

---

## Change or Adoption Lead

### Role Summary
The Change or Adoption Lead helps people understand, adopt, and sustain new processes, tools, or workflows introduced by a project.

### Responsibilities
- Plan communications, training, stakeholder engagement, and rollout support
- Identify adoption risks and support transitions for end users or teams
- Measure readiness, usage, and adoption outcomes
- Coordinate feedback loops after launch or rollout
- Help teams build confidence in new ways of working

### How this role interacts with existing personas
- Collaborates with Product Owners and Project Managers to align change plans with value and timeline
- Partner with Technical Leads and Operations on readiness, support, and feedback collection
- Works with stakeholders to ensure the change is understood, accepted, and sustained
- Supports leadership in communicating why the change matters and how success will be measured

### Goals
- Increase adoption and sustainable behavior change
- Reduce confusion, resistance, and rollout friction
- Improve the long-term value of project outcomes
