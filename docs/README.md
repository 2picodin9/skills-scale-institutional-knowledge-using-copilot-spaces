# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

OctoAcme uses a lightweight but structured project management model designed to move from idea to delivery in a repeatable, transparent way. The lifecycle begins with project initiation, where the team validates the business problem, identifies stakeholders, defines success metrics, and creates a project one-pager before deciding whether to proceed. Once approved, the team enters planning, breaking the work into a prioritized backlog, estimating effort, clarifying acceptance criteria, and setting milestones, dependencies, and release timing.

During execution, OctoAcme emphasizes iterative delivery and continuous tracking. Teams use daily standups, weekly syncs, and milestone reviews to surface progress, blockers, and dependencies. Work is expected to be small, testable, and reviewable, with pull requests linked to issues, clear acceptance criteria, and required validation in CI before review and merge. Quality is reinforced through unit tests, integration tests, end-to-end smoke testing, security scans, and manual QA where needed.

Risk management and stakeholder communication are treated as core project practices. OctoAcme maintains a risk register to track impact, likelihood, ownership, and mitigation, and uses structured escalation paths when issues affect delivery or business outcomes. Communication is regular and role-based, with PM/Product Manager alignment, engineering standups, and stakeholder status updates to keep everyone informed and aligned around a single source of truth.

The release and deployment phase standardizes how new work moves to production, including staging validation, smoke tests, rollback readiness, and post-deploy verification. After each sprint or milestone, the team conducts retrospectives to capture lessons learned, identify improvement actions, and review follow-up items. These practices are supported by clear role definitions for Product Managers, Project Managers, Developers, QA/Testing contributors, and stakeholders to ensure accountability and effective collaboration across the project lifecycle.

## Core Process Areas

- Initiation: validate the need, align stakeholders, define outcomes and rough timeline
- Planning: create backlog, estimate scope, define milestones and Definition of Done
- Execution and Tracking: iterate in small increments, review progress, manage blockers
- Risk and Communication: monitor dependencies, escalate issues, keep stakeholders informed
- Release and Deployment: validate quality, ship safely, and verify outcomes
- Retrospectives: capture learnings and turn them into improvement actions
- Roles and Personas: clarify ownership and collaboration across the team
